# Standard intake form

Intro: Please fill in the form to the best of your abilities. Leave out items you feel you would prefer not to answer. This is really helpful in understanding how we talk with you, and how we can interact with you through the sessions. This helps us to help you have a great and comfortable experience.

Acknowledgement (checkbox, required): In completing this form and booking the appointment, I acknowledge that any treatment session provided is intended to enhance and assist in releasing mental, emotional, and/or energetic blockages and to assist in creating a new state of mind and awareness that helps in creating a better and healthier life for me. I understand that it is not a substitute for traditional medical treatment or medications. I am aware that the practitioner does not diagnose illness or disease and does not prescribe medications.
