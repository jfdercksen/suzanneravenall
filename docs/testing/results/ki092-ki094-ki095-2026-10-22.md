## KI092: cold load of a product page or /checkout

Cause: the page waits for its JS chunks before the buttons work ("Loading..." = not hydrated yet), and Node in the 1-CPU web container served those chunks. On 21 Oct, during on-box image builds, single chunks took 2.8 s and the pages hung 15 s+. On a quiet box before the fix: product 1.4 s, checkout 1.6 s (04:16Z).

Fix: the web container publishes `.next/static` into the `web_static` volume at start; nginx serves `/_next/static/` from disk (falls back to Node).

Proof:
- Served by nginx from disk: chunk responses carry nginx's ETag and `Expires: 2037`; `[publish-static] added=151 kept=0`; files owned by uid 1001.
- Inside the box (server time): product HTML 0.10-0.12 s, all 20 product-page chunks in 163 ms; one chunk 6-40 ms over 10 tries.
- Under load (the medusa deploy building on the box, load 2.3), this PC, fresh contexts, 07:41Z: 375 cold product 2.6 s / 2.45 s / 2.42 s, checkout 1.3 s; 1280 cold product 2.5 s (x3) and one outlier 8.1 s (one chunk 6.8 s, this PC's link); warm 0.4-0.5 s. Every chunk came from nginx's disk. Before the fix, under the same kind of load: 15 s+.
- Quiet, after deploy, 08:12Z: checkout cold 0.39 s (375) and 0.59 s (1280); warm product 0.38 / 0.69 s, warm checkout 0.49 / 0.54 s. Product cold 2.0-3.1 s over 8 tries: HTML commit about 1.1 s plus about 1.2 s for the chunks over this PC's link (ping 19-515 ms during the session). The server's share is about 0.3 s; the remainder is the download of the product page's JS (about 880 KB uncompressed) over this link.
- Screenshots: `browser/ki092-{375,1280}-{product,checkout}-cold.png`.

Verdict: PASS for the hang (no load over 8.1 s, typical 2.5 s under build load, was 15 s+). The 2 s target holds server-side and for /checkout; the product page cold from this PC's link is 2.0-3.1 s, bounded by the JS download. Follow-up: precompressed (gzip_static/brotli) chunks and a lighter product-page bundle.
